The predominant commercial models we apply in the market

Flexibility is key

We offer a range of flexible commercial models, often combined into a hybrid approach that best suits our clients' needs and the nature of the work.

Some of the more common commercial models include →

Fixed / output based pricing

A clear, agreed-upon cost established for well-defined deliverables and project milestones, giving budget predictability on specific outputs.

Outcome-based pricing

A portion of the fee is tied to your key business outcomes, so the engagement becomes a partnership rather than a purchase.

Retainer / FTE

Dedicated resources or committed hours allocated monthly, for consistent access to specialised expertise and proactive guidance.

Time & material

Billing against hours worked and materials consumed, for evolving needs or genuinely variable scope.

Menu card

Pricing tied transparently to specific activities or volumes, for scalable and repeatable work.
