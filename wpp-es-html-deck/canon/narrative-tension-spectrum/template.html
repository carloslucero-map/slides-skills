Living loyalty vision

What customers tell us is part of a deeper cultural shift

Not a brand-by-brand problem. Five tensions, observable across markets, generations and categories, are reshaping what loyalty even means.

  • The personalisation paradox is sharpening
    82.6% of marketers believe they make customers feel valued. 56.2% of customers agree.
  • True loyalty is becoming fragile
    Deep emotional bonds fell 5% in 2025 — the first drop in five years. Viral spikes replace durable preference.
  • AI is shifting power to the consumer
    Agents give customers more leverage to extract value, and less tolerance for friction.
  • Algorithmic fatigue is producing a counter-current
    Consumers increasingly want to be surprised. Empowerment is becoming the new personalisation.
  • Convenience is the new currency
    The brand that shows up easiest is increasingly the brand that wins.

Sources: Antavo Global Customer Loyalty Report 2026; SAP Emarsys Customer Loyalty Index 2025.
