# Logo

## The lockup

`WPP Enterprise Solutions` — the "WPP" letters are built from halftone dots, "Enterprise Solutions" is solid.
For the MAP organisation the extended lockup **`Enterprise Solutions | MAP`** is used.

Three variations exist in the wider brand system — primary single line, double stacked, triple stacked. This
system ships the **primary single-line lockup only**, in two colourways, which is all a deck needs.

## Rules

- **Colour: Navy or White only.** Navy on light grounds, White on Navy. No other colourway exists.
- **Safe space: the width of the second "P" of WPP**, on all sides.
- Sub-brand and partner logos are created centrally only. Partner co-branding runs partner logo · navy dot
  separator (two small stacked dots) · WPP ES logo, scaled to equal visual weight.

## Files

| Use | File |
|---|---|
| Light grounds | `WPP_ES_MAP_logo_NAVY.svg` — fill `#000050`, viewBox ratio about 7.42 : 1 |
| Navy grounds, and the cover badge | `WPP_ES_MAP_logo_WHITE.svg` |

Both are in the **Logos** asset group. They are build-time sources: the deck generator pastes the SVG in as an
inline `<svg>` element, and a shipped deck never references them by path. Print, CMYK and Pantone variants do
not ship here.

## Placement

In decks the logo appears small and bottom-right — either as the text footer line, or on covers inside the
solid Navy badge anchored flush to the bottom-right corner.

The contact named in the source guideline for sub-brand and partner logo creation is internal to WPP and is
deliberately not reproduced here.
