Three moves that change the work

Operating principles
Automate

The shell, furniture and locked slides come from the builder — never rebuilt by hand. Spec keys carry every sanctioned choice, so two decks differ by decision, not by accident.

Assemble

Content slides are pasted from the snippet kit and filled — layouts are chosen, not invented. Each variant is a full-canvas recipe that already passes the composition laws.

Verify

Every deck is screenshotted and checked against the guideline before it ships. What the ruler cannot judge, the art-direction pass judges by eye, slide by slide.

Chosen layouts, not invented ones, is the whole trick.
